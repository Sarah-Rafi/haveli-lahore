# Haveli Restaurant, Lahore: research notes

Gathered November 2026 from public listings. Sources sometimes disagree; conflicts are noted below.

## Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | 2170-A Food Street, Fort Road, opposite Badshahi Mosque, Shahi Mohallah, Walled City of Lahore |
| **Building** | Inside Haveli Khalil Khan, a restored old townhouse on Food Street |
| **Main draw** | Rooftop and terrace views straight onto the Badshahi Mosque; best at dusk when the mosque is lit |
| **Cuisine** | Lahori / Mughlai / Pakistani: karahi, tandoor, tawa (takka tak), Namak Mandi BBQ, desserts and chai |
| **Price level** | Mid-range, about $10–20 per person; most mains Rs 1,500–5,000 |
| **Dietary** | Halal |
| **Phone** | +92 300 8414899 (another listing gives +92 321 465 1051) |
| **Website** | haveli.com.pk |
| **Hours** | ⚠️ Conflicting: "12 pm – 12 am daily", "12 pm – 12:30 am", "Mon–Fri 1 pm – 1 am, Sat–Sun 9 am – 1 am" and "9 am – 10 pm". The site says *"around 12 pm – 12 am, call ahead"*. |
| **Tips from guides** | Book ahead for balcony tables; dress modestly near the mosque |

**Badshahi Mosque context:** commissioned by Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb and completed in 1673. It is one of the largest mosques in the world.
